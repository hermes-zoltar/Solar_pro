FAQ

Questions We
Always Answer.

Everything you want to know before committing. If your question is not here, it will be answered in your free energy audit call.

Most residential installations are completed within 48 hours. Commercial projects typically take 3 to 5 business days depending on system size and site preparation requirements. We give you a precise timeline after the energy audit.
Our hybrid systems are designed with intelligent load management. If the battery reaches a set threshold overnight, the system seamlessly draws from the grid or a generator if one is connected. You are never left without power. The system manages all of this automatically.
Systems are priced based on your specific load requirements. Residential systems start from ₦3.5M. Commercial systems are quoted after a site assessment. We do not believe in one-size pricing. Your system should fit your building, not a catalogue.
Every Helios system comes with a 10-year comprehensive warranty covering panels, inverter and battery. Panel performance is guaranteed to stay above 80% efficiency for 25 years. We also offer annual maintenance plans for commercial clients.
